# Provider facts checked 26 November 2026

Public Nominatim is not enabled. The current OSMF policy prohibits autocomplete and generic geocoding offered/generated by no-code/low-code/vibe-coding platforms without a deliberate informed responsible-developer decision. Public geocoder availability is not permission to automatically connect it. A licensed or self-hosted adapter is required here. [1]

Public OSM raster tiles permit normal human interactive viewport viewing, visible attribution, identifying native requests/browser referrer and respect for HTTP caching. They prohibit bulk/prefetch and offline-region downloads. The current implementation does not prefetch or offer downloads, uses a browser/WebView cache and visibly attributes the map. Native cache/referrer behaviour still requires device validation. [2]

OSRM hosted test services are for reasonable non-commercial use, at most one request per second, with no uptime/freshness SLA. NEXORA's server serialises requests with at least 1.1 seconds between starts across profiles. Separate car, foot and bike graphs were smoke-tested once for King's Cross → Tate Modern; resulting route samples are retained. Alternatives are requested, never guaranteed or manufactured. This is not production infrastructure or safety/legal restriction validation. [3] [4]

The configurable geocoder is a Nominatim-compatible operator-controlled response adapter; other provider schemas require adaptation. No business photos/reviews/hours are connected. Reliability, charges, road limits, traffic and legal access coverage remain unknown.
